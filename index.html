        Budget settings

        Your starting balance and your pay cycle.

        The money you had when you started recording in PesaOtas. Your spending cash is worked out from this plus every transaction, so it carries across pay cycles by itself. Only spending from this date counts against this cycle's budget. Leave empty to count everything recorded so far.

        You'll see a summary first. Nothing is deleted, and nothing changes until you confirm.

        Currency

        The currency your budget is kept in. One currency per budget.

        PesaOtas never converts amounts between currencies. Changing the currency only changes the label shown with your amounts.

        Backup your data

        Export a copy before changing devices or clearing browser data.

        Backups include your budget, settings, currency and transaction history. Keep the file somewhere private.

        Only needed if you ever go back to PesaOtas v1.5.0. It keeps every amount, transaction, category, savings account and pay cycle. It leaves out only things v1.6 alone keeps, none of them financial: the record of currency changes, when your budget was upgraded, and alert review records.

        Savings starting balances

        What each savings account already held before you started recording savings here. This does not change your spending cash.

        Entered by hand. PesaOtas never connects to KCB M-PESA, M-Shwari or any account, and never asks for PINs or passwords.

        Storage protection

        How safely this browser is keeping your PesaOtas data.

        Your data can still be lost, so keep downloading backups. Even when protected, PesaOtas data is only in this browser on this device. It is gone if:

        • you clear this browser's history, cookies or site data, or uninstall the browser or the installed app;
        • you use a private or incognito window (its data is deleted when you close it);
        • the phone or computer is lost, reset or replaced;
        • on iPhone, iPad or Mac, you open PesaOtas in Safari rather than from the Home Screen and don't use it for 7 days of Safari use: Safari may delete its data. Added to the Home Screen, PesaOtas is treated as its own app.

        A downloaded backup file is the only copy that survives all of these.

        Data safety

        Copies kept on this device so nothing is lost when PesaOtas moves, restores or resets your data. PesaOtas never deletes them on its own.

        Erase everything and start again

        Removes your budget, transactions, pay cycles, savings records and alert review records from this browser and starts PesaOtas empty. A copy of your current data is kept in Data safety, but download a backup first if you might need it.

        Privacy note

        PesaOtas does not connect to your bank or M-Pesa, and does not send your financial data to a server. Anyone with access to this browser profile may be able to open the app, so protect your device and keep backups secure.
