Storage protection

How safely this browser is keeping your PesaOtas data.

Your data can still be lost, so keep downloading backups. Even when protected, PesaOtas data is only in this browser on this device. It is gone if:

  • you clear this browser's history, cookies or site data, or uninstall the browser or the installed app;
  • you use a private or incognito window (its data is deleted when you close it);
  • the phone or computer is lost, reset or replaced;
  • on iPhone, iPad or Mac, you open PesaOtas in Safari rather than from the Home Screen and don't use it for 7 days of Safari use — Safari may delete its data. Added to the Home Screen, PesaOtas is treated as its own app.

A downloaded backup file is the only copy that survives all of these.

Data safety

Copies kept on this device so nothing is lost when PesaOtas moves, restores or resets your data. PesaOtas never deletes them on its own.

Erase everything and start again

Removes your budget, transactions, pay cycles and savings records from this browser and starts PesaOtas empty. A copy of your current data is kept in Data safety, but download a backup first if you might need it.

Privacy note

PesaOtas does not connect to your bank or M-Pesa, and does not send your financial data to a server. Anyone with access to this browser profile may be able to open the app, so protect your device and keep backups secure.
